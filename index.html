About ART Mesonet Mumbai

ART Mesonet Mumbai is a high-resolution rainfall monitoring network using Automatic Rain Gauges and Doppler X-Band Radar to support flood forecasting and urban weather monitoring.

Projects

ART Mesonet Mumbai

The ART Mesonet Mumbai project is focused on strengthening high-resolution weather observation and monitoring over the Mumbai metropolitan region.

Key Components

  • Automatic Rain Gauge (ARG) network for real-time rainfall monitoring
  • Doppler X-Band Radar for high-resolution precipitation and storm analysis

Objectives

  • Improved monitoring of extreme rainfall events
  • Support for urban flood forecasting and early warning
  • Enhanced understanding of mesoscale weather systems
